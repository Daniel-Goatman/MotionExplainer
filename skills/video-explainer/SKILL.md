description: Create source-grounded Remotion video explainers in this shared workspace, from chapter references and drawn storyboards through narrated motion review and verified local delivery.

Read [the authoring guide](../../VIDEO-GENERATION.md), [design authority](../../DESIGN.md) and [pipeline](../../PIPELINE.md), then the selected job’s brief. Use the same guide in Codex and Claude. Keep all project-specific content, approvals, settings, assets, status and output mappings in that job.

Let source coverage and comprehension determine duration within the agreed ceiling. Draw a representative reference for each chapter, then actual key states and continuous bridges. A script or JSON list alone is not a storyboard. Review explanatory value, visual variety, achievable fidelity, factual grounding and legibility. Obtain storyboard approval before production, then review a representative narrated motion sample with its real sound before scaling up.

Compose effective visual models using shared SpeechControl primitives. Centre the main composition and takeaways. Keep object identity through smooth eased transitions, with no cuts. Cue emphasis, lifts, field highlights and meaningful connection pulses to narration; use restrained purposeful visual motion during explanations. Occasional draw-in highlighted takeaways and short side bullets can orient viewers in technical sections. They support, rather than replace, the visual explanation.

Record voice and sound choices per job. Review sound by listening; numerical audio checks alone do not establish taste. Keep clean stems, licensed assets and attribution. Do not use unreviewed generated noise or synthetic clicks as finished ambience/effects.

Verify actual rendered passages and transition midpoints, sources, captions, assets, timing, full decode and final playback. Technical validity is separate from creative acceptance. Never freeze explanatory motion as a rendering shortcut. Preserve prior work and report verification limits honestly.

For independent versions, read only the selected job and original research. Do not inspect or reuse another job’s creative material without explicit authorisation. Do not modify upstream design sources, change dependencies, choose paid services or publish merely because production is authorised.
